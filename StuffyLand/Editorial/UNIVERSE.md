# Stuffy Land universe overview

Stuffy Land is the magical world around Opal's bed and inside her galaxy tent. Real Opal sleeps and plays nearby while her stuffed friends experience adventures. Blankets become landscapes, pillows become castles, and the wider world holds woods, villages, floating paths, and marvelous trouble. Friendship, imagination, curiosity, bravery, kindness, forgiveness, and understanding hold this world together.

## Other established figures

Duke Dozington is the giant purple pillow-shaped Snore-Snatcher: tiny legs, four enormous arms, curled mustache, nightcap with a bell, floating pillow, and trumpet vacuum. He steals sounds to power an Ultimate Quiet Machine because he cannot sleep. The original story restores the sounds, helps him make a quiet room, and ends with his friendship with Langston.

A blue-and-black kitty outside the Aphmau collection becomes a heart monster in the recovered Halloween summary. The adaptation uses the editorial name Hearts-y, informed by a label in a source illustration; the expanded written archive does not establish her name. Do not count her among the 31. The giant cloud villain, Skykeeper, Star Eater, Inside-Out Storm, and time storm are recovered premises; their detailed new dialogue and mechanics belong to the current adaptations.

## Recurring places and objects

The galaxy tent and Opal's bed are home, comfort, and departure point. The village square is a place for gatherings. The Whispering Woods contain glowing mushrooms, a wobbly bridge, sleepy flowers, and Duke's pillow castle. The pillow fort becomes Opal's Royal Resting Castle while she is sick. The Lost Star becomes the Friendship Star through shared light. Langston's pumpkin helmet, troll cape, and mission snacks support comic continuity.

Other details in new adaptations—including a weather keeper, the memory chest's three shapes, the Star Eater's home light, and the Thousand Traps quilt—are newly written plot devices, not recovered archive facts. Future stories may revisit them deliberately without claiming they appeared in the missing originals.

## Current collection

The app contains two adventures adapted from complete source stories, one original read-along, nine read-alongs expanded from recovered summaries, and one new story based on a surviving title/premise: 13 total. Exact source status, filenames, and references appear in `story-provenance.json`.

The complete original Pillow-Fort and Snore-Snatcher texts remain in editorial sources for reference rather than duplicate linear entries in the app. The provisional Pip/Mallow/Button Hill pilot is retired from the visible catalog; it is not Stuffy Land canon.

## Open archive questions

The source phrase “asthma plushies” in the invisibility summary has no defined roster and may be a transcription error. The adaptation says “the plushies” rather than inventing a new species or medical character group. Exact older story wording, exact original endings outside the three complete stories, and some missing original images are unavailable. Preserve that uncertainty.
