Opal and the Great Stuffy Land Giggle Games

It was a perfectly ordinary morning in Stuffy Land.

Well, almost perfectly ordinary.

Langston was standing on top of a pillow wearing a saucepan as a helmet, three socks on one foot, and a blanket tied around his shoulders.

“Citizens of Stuffy Land!” he announced. “I have a very important announcement!”

Opal looked up from her breakfast.

“Are you stuck again?”

“No! This is an official royal announcement!”

Lolo snuggled beside Opal. “What are you announcing?”

Langston took a deep breath.

“Today, I declare that Stuffy Land shall host the GREATEST GIGGLE GAMES IN HISTORY!”

There was a pause.

Then Axey bounced into the room.

“GIGGLE GAMES?”

“YES!” Langston shouted. “A competition featuring ridiculous challenges, extraordinary athletic abilities, and absolutely no boring activities!”

Opal grinned.

“What kind of challenges?”

Langston unrolled a giant scroll.

It was so long that it rolled down the stairs, across the hallway, through the galaxy tent, and out the door.

At the very bottom, it said:

“RULE NUMBER ONE: EVERYBODY HAS TO HAVE FUN.”

Opal loved it.

“Let's do it!”

And so began the biggest, silliest day in Stuffy Land history.

The first challenge was the Great Obstacle Course.

Coatl volunteered to build it. He carefully arranged soft hills, little tunnels, cushion bridges, and a giant wobbly ramp.

Axey immediately volunteered to test it.

He raced through the tunnel, bounced over the cushion hills, and zoomed down the ramp.

“THAT WAS AMAZING!” he shouted.

Then he went around again.

And again.

And again.

“Axey,” Opal called, “you have to let someone else try!”

Axey skidded to a stop.

“Right! Sorry!”

Finley and Stella, the two fluffy white doodle girls, took their turn together. Finley carefully investigated every obstacle like a detective, while Stella happily trotted through the course, wagging her tail.

Finley even found a shortcut underneath the cushion bridge.

Langston immediately declared it cheating.

Finley barked.

Langston reconsidered.

“Actually, it is an extremely clever shortcut.”

Then came the Aphmau kitty team.

Cherryberry led the way, with Cinny following close behind. Turtle Kitty rolled through the tunnel while Caterpillar Kitty wiggled through every twist and turn, each colorful body segment bouncing along.

Unique Pink used her crafting skills to make a tiny ribbon finish line. Cookie zoomed across it first, followed by Banana Splitty and Snow Cone Kitty.

Water Kitty splashed through a little water obstacle, while Fire Kitty warmed a chilly patch of the course just enough to make it comfortable.

Mermaid Kitty swam through a sparkling pool, and Spirit Kitty spun through the air like a little whirlwind.

Nebula Johnny floated over the entire course using a glowing moon shield.

“HEY!” Langston shouted. “YOU CAN'T FLY OVER THE OBSTACLES!”

Nebula Johnny looked down.

Then he slowly floated back to the starting line.

“Thank you,” Langston said. “Rules are important.”

Vanilla-y and Swirly took their turns together, although they spent most of the time arguing about which flavor was better.

“Vanilla is classic,” said Vanilla-y.

“Chocolate is obviously superior,” said Swirly.

“Chocolate is messy.”

“Vanilla is boring.”

They reached the finish line at exactly the same time.

Neither would admit the other had won.

Onion Ring Kitty was so tiny that the obstacle course looked enormous. She made it through the entire thing by squeezing between the tiniest gaps.

Poison Kitty tried to convince everyone that the finish line was actually at the beginning.

Nobody believed her.

Evil Queen Kitty announced that she should automatically win because she was royalty.

Opal smiled.

“There's no automatic winning today.”

Evil Queen Kitty considered this.

Then she entered the course and tried very hard to win anyway.

Snowy made a beautiful little snow tunnel, and the Dragon Couple flew together above it, the purple boy dragon kitty and dark-blue girl dragon kitty sparkling beneath the sunlight.

Aaron-y, with his black hoodie and red highlights, teamed up with Caterpillar Kitty for a sneaky shortcut. Aphmau-y watched from the sidelines, cheering them on.

Frosted Kitty joined Langston's team.

That was a mistake.

Together, they became known as The Annoyings.

They spent most of the course distracting the other contestants by making ridiculous noises.

“BEEP BEEP!”

“MEOW MEOW!”

“POTATO!”

Nobody knew why they shouted potato.

They simply did.

The second challenge was the Great Stuffy Land Talent Show.

Opal stood on the stage with Lolo beside her.

“Everyone gets to show something they're good at,” Opal announced.

Langston immediately stepped forward.

“I WILL GO FIRST.”

He performed a dramatic dance that involved spinning, stomping, waving his arms, and falling over on purpose.

He called it the Troll Tornado.

The audience clapped.

Then Axey performed an explorer's jump so impressive that he landed inside a tiny cardboard treasure chest.

Finley demonstrated her detective skills by finding a missing cookie hidden inside Langston's pocket.

Langston looked shocked.

“I WAS SAVING THAT!”

Stella performed a perfect little twirl and finished by sitting politely.

Everyone cheered.

Coatl demonstrated his glowing shell buttons, which created a beautiful pattern of lights across the stage.

Then came the Aphmau kitties.

Unique Pink made a sparkling rainbow sculpture.

Cinny performed a cinnamon-roll balancing trick.

Cherryberry told jokes.

Turtle Kitty popped in and out of her shell so quickly that everyone lost count.

Caterpillar Kitty performed a colorful wiggle dance.

Cookie did a little hop.

Banana Splitty and Snow Cone Kitty performed a matching dessert-themed dance.

Water Kitty made tiny floating bubbles.

Fire Kitty created a warm glowing ring of light.

Snowy sprinkled snowflakes over the audience.

Mermaid Kitty performed a beautiful swimming dance.

Spirit Kitty spun in a tiny tornado.

Nebula Johnny created a moonlit star show.

Vanilla-y and Swirly performed a duet, although they argued about the song's title halfway through.

Onion Ring Kitty did a tiny bow.

Poison Kitty performed a mysterious magic trick involving a pretend potion.

Evil Queen Kitty gave a very dramatic speech about how wonderful she was.

The Dragon Couple flew together in a heart-shaped loop.

Aaron-y performed a secret-agent routine with Caterpillar Kitty.

Aphmau-y showed off some impressive moves.

And Frosted Kitty performed a donut dance with Langston.

The Annoyings were back.

Nobody was surprised.

Then Opal had an idea.

“What if we have one final challenge?”

Everyone gathered around.

“What is it?” asked Lolo.

“The Great Stuffy Land Team Dance!”

Langston gasped.

“A DANCE-OFF?”

“Not exactly,” Opal said. “Everyone has to dance together. There are no winners and no losers.”

Langston looked disappointed.

“But how will we know who's the best?”

Opal smiled.

“By how much fun we have.”

Langston thought about this.

“Acceptable.”

The music started.

Cherryberry began the rhythm.

Cinny clapped along.

The Dragon Couple flew overhead.

Water Kitty made bubbles that popped to the beat.

Fire Kitty made little glowing lights.

Snowy sprinkled snowflakes.

Spirit Kitty spun through the air.

Nebula Johnny made a sparkling moon above the dance floor.

Unique Pink tossed ribbons into the air.

Caterpillar Kitty wiggled.

Turtle Kitty bounced.

Mermaid Kitty swam in circles.

Aaron-y and Aphmau-y danced together.

Vanilla-y and Swirly tried to dance in opposite directions, but eventually bumped into each other and started laughing.

Cookie, Banana Splitty, and Snow Cone Kitty formed a little dessert dance line.

Onion Ring Kitty bounced in the middle.

Poison Kitty performed a dramatic spin.

Evil Queen Kitty danced like she was accepting an award.

Frosted Kitty and Langston did the Troll Tornado.

Finley and Stella danced side by side.

Coatl swayed gently.

Axey bounced so high that he almost touched the ceiling.

And Opal danced with Lolo right in the center.

The whole room was glowing.

Everyone was laughing.

Everyone was having fun.

Then Langston had one final idea.

He climbed onto the stage.

“ATTENTION, EVERYBODY! I HAVE CREATED A NEW DANCE MOVE!”

He demonstrated by putting his hands on his knees, sticking his tongue out, and wiggling his bottom.

“THE TROLLY WIGGLE!”

Opal burst out laughing.

Lolo giggled.

Axey fell over.

Even Evil Queen Kitty seemed amused.

Then, one by one, everyone tried the Trolly Wiggle.

The entire dance floor became a sea of wiggling plushies.

It was ridiculous.

It was wonderful.

And it was completely impossible to take seriously.

When the music finally stopped, everyone collapsed onto the soft cushions.

Langston lay on his back, staring at the ceiling.

“I am exhausted.”

“You did a lot of dancing,” Opal said.

“I also did a lot of supervising.”

“You mostly yelled potato.”

“That was part of the supervision.”

Lolo snuggled against Opal.

“I think this was the best day.”

Opal smiled.

“Me too.”

Cherryberry brought over a little golden trophy.

It had a tiny star on top and a message engraved on the front:

“THE GREATEST GIGGLE GAMES — EVERYONE WINS!”

Opal held it up.

“Because everyone made today special!”

Everyone cheered.

Then Langston raised his hand.

“Does everyone winning mean everyone gets snacks?”

Opal laughed.

“Yes.”

“AND DOES EVERYONE WINNING MEAN I GET EXTRA SNACKS?”

“No.”

Langston sighed dramatically.

“I knew this competition was rigged.”

Finley barked.

Stella wagged her tail.

Axey giggled.

And Lolo gave Opal one more cozy hug.

As the sun began to set, all the friends gathered for one enormous group photograph. Every kitty, every dog, every turtle, every dragon, every explorer, and every troll squeezed together.

Langston stood in the middle, wearing his saucepan helmet.

“Everybody say TROLLY WIGGLE!”

“TROLLY WIGGLE!”

And just as the picture was taken, Langston accidentally sneezed.

“KER-CHOOOOOO!”

His saucepan flew into the air and landed perfectly on Evil Queen Kitty's head.

Everyone laughed so hard that the photographer had to take another picture.

It was the perfect ending to the perfect day.

And from that day forward, the Great Stuffy Land Giggle Games became an annual tradition.

The only rule was that Langston wasn't allowed to design the obstacle course.

He kept putting trampolines over the snack table.

The end.
