Opal and the Great Pillow-Fort Mystery

The morning sun peeked through the galaxy tent, painting little stars across Opal's blankets.

Opal opened her eyes, stretched her arms, and gave a tiny sneeze.

“Ah-choo!”

Lolo, her soft sloth lovey, was snuggled right beside her.

“Good morning, Opal,” Lolo whispered.

“Good morning,” Opal replied, her voice a little scratchy.

She sat up, but her head felt heavy, and her body felt extra sleepy. She had a little sniffle, too.

Lolo gave her a gentle hug.

“I think today might be a cozy day.”

Opal nodded.

“I wanted to explore the forest with Axey and play games with Langston.”

“There's always tomorrow,” Lolo said. “Today we can have a different kind of adventure.”

Opal smiled, although she still wished she felt better.

Just then, Langston burst through the bedroom door.

“GOOD MORNING, TINY PEOPLE! WHO'S READY FOR THE MOST INCREDIBLE, AMAZING, ENORMOUS ADVENTURE IN STUFFY LAND HISTORY?”

Opal giggled, then coughed.

Langston stopped.

“Whoa. You sound like a frog who swallowed a kazoo.”

“Thanks, Langston.”

“I'm very good at medical observations.”

Lolo gave him a look.

“Maybe you could use a quieter voice today.”

Langston immediately whispered at full volume.

“UNDERSTOOD.”

Opal laughed.

Langston noticed her blanket and the little tissue beside her pillow.

“Are you feeling sick?”

“Just a little under the weather,” Opal said.

Langston's expression softened.

“Oh. Well, then, I have decided that today's adventure will be brought directly to YOU.”

He struck a heroic pose.

“THE ADVENTURE DELIVERY SERVICE IS NOW OPEN!”

A little while later, Opal was tucked into her blankets with Lolo beside her. A tray held some water, tissues, and a few crackers.

Then came a knock.

“Special delivery!” called Axey.

He entered carrying a tiny explorer's backpack.

Behind him came Finley and Stella, the two fluffy white doodle girls. Finley had a notebook in her mouth, while Stella carried a soft blanket that was almost as big as she was.

Coatl followed slowly, carrying a basket of smooth stones.

“What's all this?” Opal asked.

Axey grinned.

“We're bringing the adventure to you!”

Langston marched in last, carrying a pillow so enormous that he couldn't see over it.

“MAKE WAY! I HAVE ARRIVED WITH THE MOST IMPORTANT EQUIPMENT!”

He dropped the pillow.

“FWOOOOMP!”

It landed on the floor and bounced once.

“Pillow delivery complete.”

Opal laughed.

The friends began building the biggest pillow fort Stuffy Land had ever seen. They used blankets, cushions, stuffed animals, and even Langston's giant troll cape.

Finley supervised construction with her detective notebook.

Stella tested every cushion by lying on it.

“She's doing quality control,” Axey explained.

“She's doing a lot of lying down,” Langston observed.

Stella wagged her tail.

The fort grew into a wonderful little castle with a blanket roof, soft walls, and a tiny entrance just big enough for Opal and Lolo.

When it was finished, Langston placed a sign over the entrance:

“OPAL'S ROYAL RESTING CASTLE.”

Underneath, in smaller letters, he had written:

“LANGSTON IS ALSO ALLOWED INSIDE, PROBABLY.”

Just as everyone settled in, Cherryberry arrived with the Aphmau plushies.

They brought decorations, little pretend treats, and a special surprise.

Unique Pink had made a tiny purple crown for Opal.

Snowy had created a miniature snowflake decoration.

Cinny brought a cinnamon-roll-shaped pillow.

And Frosted Kitty carried a little pretend donut.

“Welcome to the royal castle!” Cherryberry announced.

Opal placed the crown on her head.

“I feel very fancy.”

“You look very fancy,” Lolo agreed.

Then something strange happened.

A little golden light appeared inside the fort.

It floated above Opal's pillow, spun around twice, and turned into a tiny glowing map.

Everyone stared.

Axey leaned forward.

“Is that a treasure map?”

The map showed the galaxy tent, the pillow fort, and a little golden star at the far end of a winding path.

At the bottom were the words:

“THE LOST STAR OF STUFFY LAND.”

Langston gasped.

“WE HAVE A MYSTERY!”

Opal looked at the map.

“I want to solve it!”

Lolo gently touched her paw.

“You can help from right here.”

Opal thought about that.

“Can I be the mission commander?”

“Absolutely,” said Cherryberry.

Langston saluted.

“COMMANDER OPAL, YOUR CREW AWAITS!”

The map showed that the Lost Star was hidden somewhere in the pillow fort.

The friends began searching.

Axey explored the cushion tunnels.

Finley sniffed around the entrance.

Stella checked beneath the blankets.

Coatl examined the glowing map.

Langston took charge of the most important task.

“Searching for treasure requires snacks.”

He disappeared into the kitchen.

A few minutes later, he returned with a plate.

“MISSION SNACKS HAVE ARRIVED!”

Lolo looked at the plate.

“Those are mostly cookies.”

“Exactly. Highly advanced mission snacks.”

Opal giggled.

She studied the map carefully.

“Wait. The star on the map moves whenever someone walks near the fort.”

Everyone stopped.

The golden star moved again.

“It isn't showing us where the star is,” Opal said. “It's showing us where the star isn't!”

Axey blinked.

“That's confusing.”

“Look,” Opal explained. “Every time we search a place, the star moves away from it. We need to look somewhere we haven't searched.”

Finley wagged her tail.

Stella barked.

Coatl turned toward the tallest pillow tower.

“Perhaps there.”

Everyone looked.

At the very top of the tower was a tiny golden sparkle.

“THERE IT IS!” Langston shouted.

He charged toward the tower.

“LANGSTON!” Opal called. “Careful!”

He climbed onto the first cushion.

Then the second.

Then the third.

The tower wobbled.

“EVERYTHING IS UNDER CONTROL!” he announced.

The entire tower leaned sideways.

“FWOOOOOMP!”

Langston slid down the cushions and landed in a giant pile of blankets.

His pumpkin helmet rolled across the floor.

“MISSION SUCCESSFUL,” he announced from underneath the blankets.

Axey laughed.

“Did you get the star?”

Langston lifted his head.

“No. But I found a very comfortable blanket.”

Opal giggled so hard that she had to pause and take a little rest.

Lolo tucked the blanket around her.

“Commander, perhaps we should send a smaller explorer.”

Axey raised his paw.

“THAT'S ME!”

He climbed the pillow tower carefully, with Coatl steadying the bottom and Finley and Stella watching from below.

At the top, Axey reached behind a cushion and pulled out a tiny golden star.

He held it high.

“FOUND IT!”

The fort glowed.

Golden lights spread across the blankets, and little stars appeared on the ceiling.

The lost star had been found!

But the map wasn't finished.

A second message appeared beneath the first.

“THE STAR SHINES BRIGHTEST WHEN EVERYONE SHARES ITS LIGHT.”

Opal looked at it.

“What does that mean?”

Cherryberry thought for a moment.

“Maybe everyone needs to hold the star together.”

The friends gathered around Axey.

He placed the little golden star in the center of the fort.

Opal reached out from her blankets and touched it.

Lolo placed her paw beside Opal's.

Then Axey, Langston, Coatl, Finley, Stella, Cherryberry, and all the other plushies joined in.

The star glowed brighter and brighter.

Suddenly, a beautiful little galaxy appeared above the fort. Tiny planets floated around the ceiling, and sparkling constellations connected the friends with glowing golden lines.

The Lost Star had become the Friendship Star.

And right in the middle of the galaxy, a message appeared:

“EVERYONE HAS SOMETHING SPECIAL TO SHARE. SOME DAYS IT'S COURAGE. SOME DAYS IT'S KINDNESS. AND SOME DAYS IT'S RESTING WHILE YOUR FRIENDS HELP.”

Opal smiled.

“I like that.”

Lolo snuggled closer.

“Me too.”

Langston looked at the message.

“Does it say anything about snacks?”

The galaxy sparkled.

A tiny golden cookie appeared above his head.

Langston gasped.

“THE STAR UNDERSTANDS ME!”

Everyone laughed.

As afternoon turned into evening, Opal started feeling sleepy again.

The adventure had been wonderful, but she was ready for a nap.

The friends quietly tidied the fort and made sure Opal had everything she needed.

Finley placed her detective notebook beside the pillow.

Stella curled up near the foot of the bed.

Coatl settled nearby, keeping watch.

The Aphmau plushies arranged the little golden star where Opal could see it.

Axey whispered, “We can have another adventure when you're feeling better.”

Opal smiled.

“Promise?”

“Promise,” said Axey.

Langston leaned over the bed.

“And when you're better, I have planned an adventure involving a giant troll, a trampoline, and approximately four hundred marshmallows.”

Opal gave him a sleepy smile.

“Sounds like a lot.”

“IT WILL BE.”

Lolo gently pulled the blanket up.

“Let's let our commander rest.”

Langston nodded and whispered, “Goodnight, Commander.”

Opal closed her eyes.

Just before she drifted off, she felt Lolo's soft paw in hers.

“You know what I learned today?” Opal whispered.

“What?” Lolo asked.

“An adventure doesn't always mean going somewhere. Sometimes it means having your friends bring the fun to you.”

Lolo smiled.

“And sometimes the bravest thing you can do is give yourself time to feel better.”

Opal snuggled closer.

Above the pillow fort, the Friendship Star glowed softly, filling the galaxy tent with warm golden light.

And all around her, her friends settled in for a peaceful night.

Langston was the last one awake.

He carefully placed a tiny sign outside the fort:

“COMMANDER OPAL IS RESTING. PLEASE ENTER QUIETLY.”

Then he added a second sign underneath:

“NO TROLL SHOUTING.”

He looked at it proudly.

“Perfect.”

A moment later, he whispered:

“GOODNIGHT, EVERYBODY!”

Lolo opened one eye.

“Langston.”

“Sorry.”

And Stuffy Land drifted into a cozy, star-filled night.

The end.
