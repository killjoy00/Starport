Opal and TITANUS: The Biggest Bad Guy Stuffy Land Had Ever Seen

It was a beautiful morning in Stuffy Land.

The birds were singing. The flowers were sparkling. The clouds looked like giant marshmallows.

And Langston was stuck upside down in a tree.

“OPAL!”

Opal looked up from her breakfast. Lolo was snuggled beside her.

“What?”

“I HAVE A VERY IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT!”

“Are you stuck?”

“THAT IS COMPLETELY UNRELATED!”

Opal walked outside.

There was Langston, dangling from a branch by one foot. His blanket cape was wrapped around his head, and his saucepan helmet was hanging from a smaller branch.

Axey bounced over.

“How did you get up there?”

Langston looked offended.

“I was testing a new troll climbing technique.”

“What happened?”

“I climbed successfully. Then I kept climbing.”

Lolo giggled.

Coatl lumbered over and gently lowered the branch until Langston could hop down.

“Thank you,” Langston said. “I meant to do that.”

Finley and Stella, the two fluffy white doodle girls, came running over. Finley immediately opened her detective notebook, while Stella enthusiastically sniffed Langston’s saucepan helmet.

“Case closed,” Langston declared. “The tree attacked me.”

Opal laughed.

It was going to be a perfectly ordinary day.

Until the sky turned black.

Not nighttime black.

Not storm-cloud black.

ENORMOUS, SOMETHING-IS-COMING black.

The birds stopped singing.

The flowers folded their petals.

The ground began to shake.

THUUUUUUUUM.

A second tremor rattled the meadow.

THUUUUUUUUM.

Then a gigantic shadow passed over the sun.

Opal looked up.

Something enormous was approaching Stuffy Land.

Something so big that its head was higher than the tallest mountains.

Something so big that its footsteps made the lakes ripple.

Something so big that even Langston stopped talking.

A gigantic creature emerged from beyond the mountains.

It had enormous stone shoulders, glowing purple eyes, arms like bridges, and a body covered in dark armor. A crown of jagged crystals stretched across its head. Its footsteps left enormous glowing footprints in the ground.

And hanging around its neck was a giant golden bell.

The creature leaned down.

Its face filled the entire sky.

Then it spoke.

“I AM TITANUS.”

Its voice rumbled through every corner of Stuffy Land.

“THE GREATEST GIANT IN ALL THE REALMS.”

Langston slowly looked at Opal.

“That’s a pretty big guy.”

Titanus raised one enormous hand.

“I HAVE COME TO COLLECT THE HEART OF STUFFY LAND.”

Opal frowned.

“The what?”

Titanus pointed toward the center of the meadow, where a beautiful glowing crystal floated above a little pedestal.

The Heart of Stuffy Land.

It was the source of the magic that connected all the stuffies, their adventures, and their friendships.

“GIVE IT TO ME,” Titanus thundered, “AND I SHALL BECOME THE MOST POWERFUL CREATURE IN EXISTENCE!”

Langston raised his hand.

“Question.”

Titanus looked down.

“What?”

“Do you have snacks?”

Everyone stared at Langston.

Titanus growled.

“SILENCE, TINY TROLL!”

Langston lowered his hand.

“Okay. Definitely a bad guy.”

The Giant’s First Attack

Opal immediately gathered everyone in the meadow.

Cherryberry arrived first, leading the Aphmau kitty crew.

Cinny hurried in with a little cinnamon-roll snack tucked under one paw.

Turtle Kitty peeked out of her shell.

The Dragon Couple flew overhead, the completely purple boy dragon kitty and his dark-blue companion with her light-blue gem.

Aaron-y arrived in his black hoodie, gray-and-black fur and red highlights, with Aphmau-y right beside him.

Caterpillar Kitty wiggled up to Opal, every rainbow-colored segment moving independently.

Unique Pink brought her craft basket.

Frosted Kitty rolled in, already looking for Langston.

“ANNoying team meeting?” Frosted Kitty asked.

Langston grinned.

“The Annoyings are officially on duty!”

Cookie bounced over.

Banana Splitty and Snow Cone Kitty followed.

Water Kitty splashed into the meadow fountain.

Fire Kitty warmed the chilly air.

Poison Kitty carefully carried her mysterious bottle.

Snowy made tiny snowflakes appear.

Nebula Johnny floated above the group with his moon shield.

C arrived with her pink fur, black paws and tail, and pretty bows.

Mermaid Kitty swam through a ribbon of water.

Spirit Kitty spun through the air.

Vanilla-y and Swirly arrived together, arguing as usual.

“Chocolate is clearly better,” Swirly insisted.

“Vanilla is timeless,” Vanilla-y replied.

Onion Ring Kitty was so tiny that she rode on Cherryberry’s shoulder.

Evil Queen Kitty arrived wearing her purple-and-silver tiara.

And Coatl stood beside Opal, his glowing underside shell buttons shining.

Opal looked around at her friends.

“Okay, everybody. We have a REALLY big problem.”

Langston pointed toward Titanus.

“Actually, I think we have a REALLY, REALLY, REALLY big problem.”

Titanus lifted his foot.

It was enormous.

He brought it down near the meadow.

KABOOOOOOOOOM!

The ground shook.

A giant wave of dirt and leaves rolled toward the group.

Coatl stepped forward and planted his feet.

His shell buttons glowed.

A protective dome of warm light spread around everyone.

The wave rolled harmlessly around them.

“Thanks, Coatl!” Opal shouted.

Titanus frowned.

“INTERESTING.”

He raised his enormous bell.

DOOOOOOOOOONG!

The sound blasted across Stuffy Land.

Every flower folded.

Every tree bent.

Every little building shook.

And suddenly, all the stuffies began floating upward!

“WHOA!” Axey shouted.

Opal grabbed Lolo.

Langston grabbed Frosted Kitty.

Finley grabbed Stella.

Coatl anchored himself with his powerful legs.

But the others were floating higher and higher.

Titanus laughed.

“MY BELL CONTROLS GRAVITY! SOON, YOUR ENTIRE LAND WILL FLOAT INTO MY HANDS!”

Opal looked at the bell.

Then at the crystal around Titanus’s neck.

Then at the enormous creature himself.

She had an idea.

But first, they needed to get everyone safely back on the ground.

“NEBULA JOHNNY!” Opal called. “Can your moon shield protect everyone?”

Nebula Johnny expanded his shield.

A glowing moon-shaped bubble surrounded the group.

The floating stopped.

“WATER KITTY! CAN YOU HELP US GET DOWN?”

Water Kitty created a gentle stream of water that carried everyone toward the meadow.

Fire Kitty warmed the air beneath them, helping guide their descent.

The Dragon Couple flew around, helping anyone who drifted away.

Spirit Kitty created a little whirlwind that gently pushed the last few friends toward Coatl’s protective dome.

Everyone landed safely.

Langston stood up and straightened his cape.

“I would like to formally complain about the giant.”

The Secret of Titanus

Opal studied the giant from behind a hill.

He was enormous.

His footsteps shook the ground.

His bell controlled gravity.

His armor seemed almost impossible to break.

And the Heart of Stuffy Land was exactly what he wanted.

“How are we supposed to stop something that big?” Axey asked.

Lolo held Opal’s paw.

“Maybe we should find out why he wants the Heart.”

Opal nodded.

“Good idea.”

Finley began sniffing around the area where Titanus had stepped.

She discovered something strange: tiny golden pieces scattered across the ground.

She showed them to Opal.

“Good detective work, Finley!”

Stella wagged her tail and found another piece.

Opal examined the fragments.

They looked like pieces of an old golden bell.

Coatl’s shell buttons began glowing faster.

He seemed to recognize the symbol engraved on one of the pieces.

“Do you know what it means?” Opal asked.

Coatl nodded slowly.

He pointed toward the mountains.

There, hidden behind a waterfall, was an ancient picture carved into the stone.

It showed a giant holding a glowing crystal.

Below him were hundreds of smaller creatures.

The giant was protecting them.

Another picture showed the giant standing alone.

And a final picture showed the golden bell hanging around his neck.

Lolo studied it.

“I think Titanus used to be a guardian.”

Opal looked closer.

The ancient picture showed a heart-shaped crystal, just like the Heart of Stuffy Land.

“Maybe he lost something,” Opal said.

Axey bounced excitedly.

“AN ANCIENT MYSTERY!”

Aaron-y examined the symbols.

Aphmau-y helped him decipher the markings.

Together, they discovered a message:

When the little lights disappear, the giant will seek the brightest heart.

Opal thought about that.

“Maybe Titanus thinks taking our Heart will bring something back.”

Langston crossed his arms.

“Still doesn’t give him permission to stomp around our house.”

“True,” Opal said.

Then she smiled.

“We need to talk to him.”

The Biggest Rescue Mission Ever

The team split into groups.

Opal, Lolo, Axey, Langston, Aaron-y, and Aphmau-y would approach Titanus.

Coatl, Finley, and Stella would investigate the old mountain carvings.

The Dragon Couple would scout from above.

Cherryberry would organize the Aphmau kitty team.

Everyone had an important job.

Unique Pink made special glowing ribbons.

C used her charming bows to create a distraction.

Snowy prepared a giant snowstorm.

Water Kitty and Mermaid Kitty worked together to create a sparkling water path.

Fire Kitty prepared warm glowing lights.

Nebula Johnny kept his moon shield ready.

Spirit Kitty practiced a powerful whirlwind.

Vanilla-y and Swirly prepared a giant dessert-shaped decoy.

Cinny packed snacks for the mission.

Cookie, Banana Splitty, and Snow Cone Kitty helped carry supplies.

Turtle Kitty volunteered for the tiniest tunnels.

Poison Kitty prepared a harmless pretend potion that would make the decoy smell like a giant strawberry.

Evil Queen Kitty insisted on being in charge of the royal distraction.

Onion Ring Kitty volunteered for the most important job of all:

Sneaking through a tiny opening to reach the giant’s bell mechanism.

Langston stared at her.

“You’re going to sneak into THAT?”

Onion Ring Kitty nodded.

Langston saluted.

“Brave tiny warrior.”

Frosted Kitty joined Langston.

“The Annoyings are ready.”

Langston grinned.

“Let’s go annoy a giant.”

Titanus’s Fortress

The group reached Titanus’s enormous fortress.

It was built into the side of a mountain.

Its doors were taller than a hundred trees.

Its windows glowed purple.

And right at the top was the golden bell.

Titanus sat on a giant stone throne.

His enormous hands were wrapped around the Heart of Stuffy Land, which he had somehow managed to steal while the team was preparing.

Opal gasped.

“He already has it!”

Titanus looked down.

“YOU HAVE COME TO WITNESS MY GREATNESS.”

Opal stepped forward.

“Why do you want our Heart?”

Titanus leaned closer.

“BECAUSE I HAVE NO HEART OF MY OWN.”

The room went quiet.

He pointed toward a dark space behind his throne.

There was a giant empty cradle made of stone.

Once, it had held a glowing crystal.

“LONG AGO, I WAS THE GUARDIAN OF THE LITTLE STAR.”

His voice became softer.

“IT WAS MY FRIEND. MY LIGHT. MY ONLY COMPANION.”

Opal listened.

“THE STAR DISAPPEARED. I SEARCHED FOR CENTURIES. I FOUND YOUR HEART, AND I THOUGHT IT COULD REPLACE MINE.”

Lolo stepped forward.

“But it can’t replace your friend.”

Titanus growled.

“THEN I WILL TAKE IT ANYWAY!”

He raised his bell.

DOOOOOOOOOONG!

The entire fortress shook.

The group floated upward again.

“NOW!” Opal shouted.

The plan began.

The Dragon Couple swooped through the enormous windows.

The purple boy dragon kitty flew around Titanus’s head while the dark-blue girl dragon kitty with her light-blue gem flew toward the bell.

Titanus tried to swat them away.

But they were too quick.

Nebula Johnny expanded his moon shield around the group.

Spirit Kitty created a whirlwind.

Snowy unleashed a snowstorm that covered Titanus’s feet.

Water Kitty and Mermaid Kitty sent a giant wave through the fortress.

Fire Kitty created a warm glowing path across the floor.

Titanus stumbled.

THUUUUUUUUUM!

His enormous body crashed into the wall.

Langston and Frosted Kitty raced toward the throne.

“HEY, GIANT!”

Titanus looked down.

Langston was wearing his saucepan helmet again.

“YOU HAVE SOMETHING ON YOUR SHIRT!”

Titanus looked down.

Frosted Kitty tossed a giant donut decoy toward him.

It landed on his shoulder.

Titanus blinked.

“What is this?”

Langston shouted:

“IT’S A DONUT OF DISTRACTION!”

Titanus swatted at it.

Meanwhile, Onion Ring Kitty squeezed through a tiny opening near the bell mechanism.

She found a small golden lever.

She tugged.

Nothing.

She tugged harder.

Still nothing.

Then she called for help.

Caterpillar Kitty wiggled through the opening and wrapped several colorful segments around the lever.

Together, they pulled.

CLICK!

The bell’s glow flickered.

Aaron-y spotted the change.

“Aphmau-y! The bell mechanism!”

Aphmau-y nodded.

They rushed toward the control panel.

Unique Pink’s glowing ribbons secured the loose wires.

C danced across the floor, her bows sparkling and distracting the giant’s attention.

Evil Queen Kitty stood on a broken piece of throne.

“ATTENTION, TITANUS! YOU ARE HEREBY ORDERED TO STOP BEING SO ENORMOUSLY RUDE!”

Titanus looked at her.

“WHO DARES?”

“I DO!”

He reached toward her.

She ducked behind Coatl.

“ROYAL STRATEGIC RETREAT!”

Coatl’s shell buttons flashed.

A golden protective barrier appeared around the group.

Finley and Stella arrived with Coatl, carrying the last golden fragment from the mountains.

Finley placed it in Opal’s paw.

Opal looked at the fragment.

It matched the empty cradle behind Titanus’s throne.

“The little star!” she shouted.

“It’s still here!”

The Little Star

Opal ran toward the empty cradle.

Titanus noticed.

“DO NOT TOUCH THAT!”

He reached down with one enormous hand.

Coatl stepped between them.

His glowing buttons blazed.

The protective light held Titanus back for just long enough.

Opal placed the golden fragment into the cradle.

Nothing happened.

She tried turning it.

Still nothing.

Lolo looked at the ancient carvings.

“Opal, remember what the message said? The giant seeks the brightest heart.”

Opal looked at the Heart of Stuffy Land.

Then at the empty cradle.

Then at all her friends.

“Maybe the star doesn’t need a replacement heart. Maybe it needs all of us.”

She turned toward the others.

“Everybody! Bring your light!”

Cherryberry and Cinny stepped forward.

The Dragon Couple flew together, their colors shining.

Aaron-y and Aphmau-y joined hands.

Unique Pink raised her sparkling ribbons.

Caterpillar Kitty wiggled her rainbow segments.

Frosted Kitty and Langston stood shoulder to shoulder.

Cookie, Banana Splitty, and Snow Cone Kitty joined the circle.

Water Kitty, Fire Kitty, Snowy, and Mermaid Kitty added their magic.

Nebula Johnny raised his moon shield.

Spirit Kitty spun a gentle whirlwind.

C danced, her bows sparkling.

Vanilla-y and Swirly stood together, finally agreeing to stop arguing for one minute.

Poison Kitty placed her harmless potion beside the cradle.

Turtle Kitty peeked out of her shell.

Evil Queen Kitty raised her tiara.

Onion Ring Kitty stood at the very front.

Finley and Stella sat beside Coatl.

Axey bounced into the circle.

Lolo held Opal’s paw.

And Langston?

Langston raised both arms.

“EVERYBODY GIVE THE BIGGEST TROLLY CHEER YOU HAVE!”

Everyone cheered.

The Heart of Stuffy Land began glowing.

A warm golden light spread from Opal’s hand into the cradle.

The golden fragment floated upward.

A tiny star appeared.

Then another.

Then hundreds.

The little star was returning!

Titanus stared.

His purple eyes widened.

“MY STAR…”

The tiny star floated toward him.

It landed gently on his enormous hand.

Titanus’s expression changed.

For the first time, the giant smiled.

A single golden tear rolled down his cheek.

It was so enormous that it landed like a little pond.

Langston stared.

“That’s a big tear.”

Opal smiled.

“Some feelings are big.”

Titanus gently held the little star.

“I THOUGHT I HAD LOST YOU FOREVER.”

The star glowed brighter.

It had been hiding inside the Heart of Stuffy Land all along, waiting for someone to bring it back together.

Titanus looked at Opal.

“YOU COULD HAVE DEFEATED ME.”

Opal nodded.

“Probably.”

Langston whispered, “Eventually.”

“But we wanted to understand you first,” Opal continued. “You can’t take something that belongs to everyone just because you miss something.”

Titanus lowered his head.

“I AM SORRY.”

The little star floated above his shoulder.

The giant’s purple armor began changing.

The dark crystals softened into warm golden stone.

His eyes became gentle.

His enormous bell stopped glowing.

And the ground stopped shaking.

The biggest bad guy Stuffy Land had ever seen had become its biggest new friend.

The Giant-Sized Celebration

Titanus was still enormous.

VERY enormous.

He was still taller than the mountains.

But now he was careful.

He bent down to speak to Opal.

“May I visit your land?”

Opal smiled.

“Of course! But you have to be careful where you step.”

Titanus nodded seriously.

“I SHALL BE THE MOST CAREFUL GIANT IN HISTORY.”

Langston raised his hand.

“Can you help me build a treehouse?”

Titanus looked at him.

“A treehouse?”

“A GIANT treehouse.”

Titanus smiled.

“YES.”

Langston gasped.

“WE ARE GOING TO NEED A LOT OF WOOD.”

Frosted Kitty nodded.

“And donuts.”

“Obviously,” Langston said.

Everyone returned to Stuffy Land.

Titanus carefully stepped over the mountains, walked around the lakes, and lowered himself into the meadow.

The little star floated above his shoulder.

Opal organized a giant welcome party.

Unique Pink decorated the meadow with glowing ribbons.

Cherryberry and Cinny arranged the food.

Water Kitty made a sparkling fountain.

Fire Kitty lit the party lanterns.

Snowy created a beautiful snowflake display.

Nebula Johnny hung his moon shield above the dance floor.

Spirit Kitty made a gentle breeze.

Mermaid Kitty filled the little pond with sparkling water.

The Dragon Couple flew in loops around Titanus’s head.

Aaron-y and Aphmau-y performed a secret-agent dance.

Caterpillar Kitty led a rainbow wiggle parade.

Cookie, Banana Splitty, and Snow Cone Kitty created a dessert line.

Vanilla-y and Swirly finally agreed to make one giant ice-cream cake together.

Poison Kitty served pretend potions that tasted like strawberry lemonade.

Evil Queen Kitty gave a royal welcome speech.

Turtle Kitty rolled around the dance floor.

Onion Ring Kitty rode on Titanus’s enormous shoulder.

Finley and Stella played fetch with a ball the size of a pumpkin.

Coatl and Titanus stood together, the two guardians watching over everyone.

Axey climbed onto a giant mushroom and shouted:

“LET’S HAVE A GIANT GAME!”

Langston immediately jumped forward.

“THE GIANT TROLLY OBSTACLE COURSE!”

Opal laughed.

“What are the rules?”

Langston unrolled a giant scroll.

“Rule one: Titanus has to crawl.”

Titanus looked down.

“I AM VERY LARGE.”

“Exactly,” Langston said. “That’s the challenge.”

The course began.

Titanus crawled beneath the first arch.

He carefully stepped over the mushroom bridge.

He balanced on a giant pillow.

Then he reached the final obstacle: a tiny little tunnel.

He stared at it.

Langston stared at him.

Opal stared at him.

Lolo giggled.

Titanus gently lowered one finger into the tunnel.

It got stuck.

He tried another finger.

That got stuck too.

Then he slowly pulled both fingers out.

POP!

The tunnel bounced into the air and landed on Langston’s head.

Langston blinked.

“THE TUNNEL HAS CHOSEN ME.”

Everyone burst out laughing.

Even Titanus laughed.

His laugh was so enormous that it made the clouds wiggle.

HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA!

The little star above his shoulder sparkled brighter with every laugh.

Opal danced with Lolo.

Axey bounced beside them.

Finley and Stella wagged their tails.

Coatl gave a happy little nod.

Langston and Frosted Kitty performed the Troll Tornado.

And the entire Stuffy Land crew joined in.

At the end of the party, Titanus carefully placed the Heart of Stuffy Land back on its pedestal.

“I understand now,” he said. “A heart becomes stronger when it is shared.”

Opal smiled.

“Exactly.”

Titanus looked around at all the friends.

“And perhaps I have learned something else.”

“What?” asked Lolo.

The giant smiled.

“Even the biggest creature in the world needs friends.”

Langston raised his hand.

“And snacks.”

Titanus nodded.

“AND SNACKS.”

Everyone cheered.

Then Titanus leaned down and whispered to Langston:

“Would you like to help me build that treehouse tomorrow?”

Langston’s eyes widened.

“YES!”

“Then bring your best troll tools.”

Langston looked at Opal.

“I’m going to need a bigger saucepan.”

Opal laughed.

“Goodnight, Langston.”

“Goodnight, Opal!”

As the stars appeared above Stuffy Land, the little star settled beside Titanus’s shoulder.

It glowed warmly.

And far beyond the mountains, something even bigger moved in the darkness.

A gigantic shadow.

A second bell.

A voice whispered:

“TITANUS HAS FOUND HIS STAR. NOW I SHALL FIND MINE.”

But that was an adventure for another day.

For tonight, Stuffy Land was safe, everyone was together, and the biggest giant in the world had finally found his friends.

The end.
