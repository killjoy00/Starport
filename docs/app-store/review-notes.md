# StarPort App Review notes (draft)

StarPort is an interactive, original science-fiction reading app **only for ages 13+**. Its prose is written in an accessible style; the age gate remains 13+.

On first launch select **I'm 13 or older** to enter the app. No registration or demo account is required. Open **The Door That Opens Tomorrow** from the story library. There are 37 decisions on every completed route, five permanent endings, and twelve short detours that can be rewound. Progress and achievements are local. The reader can switch Dark/Light/System in Settings.

**Check for new adventures** downloads validated story JSON from the developer's public GitHub repository. The included story runs offline.

The app contains Google Mobile Ads and Apple StoreKit 2 integrations, but production ad requests are disabled in version 0.2.0; the Remove Ads product `pro.starport.story.remove_ads` has not yet been configured for sale. No payment is needed to play. There is no login, chat, user-generated content or player account.

This app is not in the Kids category. The planned App Store age rating is 13+ via the higher age rating override.

Review contact phone and name: developer must enter verified contact details in App Store Connect.
