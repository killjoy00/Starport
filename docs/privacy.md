# StarPort privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

StarPort is intended **only for users aged 13 and older**. We don't ask for an account, name, date of birth, or email address.

## Information on your device

StarPort saves reading progress, story choices, unlocked achievements, appearance settings and your self-attestation that you are at least 13 locally on your device. Downloaded story packs are also stored locally. There is no developer-operated account or progress-sync server. We do not receive these local records.

## Downloading adventures

The included adventure can be read offline. If you select **Check for new adventures**, StarPort contacts the developer's public GitHub repository over HTTPS to download a catalog and story-data files. GitHub and its network providers may process routine request information such as an IP address or request metadata under their own terms. Your individual choices and achievements are not included in story downloads.

## Advertising

The current release, StarPort 0.2.0, contains Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform software, but **live advertising is disabled in production**. Internal developer builds can show Google test ads.

If advertising is enabled in a future release, Google may process technical information such as IP addresses, device or account identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, security and measurement. We will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before enabling live ads. Planned settings for StarPort's 13+ audience include teen treatment, general-rated ads and non-personalized requests.

## Purchases

StarPort includes an Apple StoreKit 2 integration for an optional, one-time **Remove Ads** purchase. It is not yet configured as a live purchasable product. When enabled, Apple handles purchases, restores, refunds and payment details. We do not process credit-card details or run our own payment service. The app checks the entitlement on your device.

## Other features

StarPort currently has no chat, social profiles, user-generated content, or developer-operated analytics account. We don't sell reader data. To erase your local progress and settings, delete the app and its data from your device. Purchases, if offered, are managed by your Apple Account.

We will review this policy whenever the app's SDKs or privacy practices change.
