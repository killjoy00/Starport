# StarPort support

StarPort is an iOS adventure-reading app for people aged 13 and older.

**Email:** killjoy00@yahoo.com (subject: StarPort support). This is the public contact also used by the developer's EZ Trivia app.

For feedback or bugs, you may also [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/killjoy00/Starport/issues/new). Please do not include personal information or purchase details in public issues.

## Questions

**Where is my saved story?** Progress and achievements stay on the device. There is no account or cross-device syncing.

**Can I undo an ending?** The twelve short detours can be rewound. After any of the five final endings, begin a new run; discovered endings remain on your shelf.

**How can I get new adventures?** Tap **Check for new adventures** in the library. Previously downloaded stories work offline.

**How can I restore a purchase?** Once the non-consumable Remove Ads product is available, use **Settings → Restore purchases** on the Apple Account used to buy it.

**Who can use StarPort?** Only users aged 13 and older. The straightforward science-fiction writing style does not change the minimum age.
